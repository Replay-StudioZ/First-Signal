# Custom IK & Free Look Mechanics (Archive)

> **Archived prototype.** Development has officially moved to **Unreal Engine 5 (C++)**.

We decided to archive this repository and migrate the project to Unreal Engine 5 to avoid the limitations and risks of a closed third-party ecosystem, gain full control over our infrastructure, and focus on a deeper, story-driven experience with custom physics and graphics.

### What's inside:
- Custom procedural **Inverse Kinematics (IK)** solver
- Decoupled **Free Look** camera controller
- Built with **Luau** & **Rojo**
